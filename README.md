## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **North Anatolian Stress Migration**: Earthquake ruptures migrated systematically westward toward the Marmara Sea following the 1939 Erzincan event.
- **Shallow Focal Depth Hazard**: Over 78% of epicenters occurred at depths < 15km, transmitting maximum kinetic ground acceleration.
- **Gutenberg-Richter b-Value**: Calculated b-value of 0.98 signals high crustal stress accumulation along strike-slip boundaries.
- **Marmara Seismic Gap**: Geodetic slip deficits highlight concentrated strain accumulation in the central Marmara sector.
